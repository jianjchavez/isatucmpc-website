What we collect

The information in this application: your name, date and place of birth, sex, civil status, addresses, contact numbers, email, TIN, ID number, employment, photo and signature. It also includes the names, birth dates and contact numbers of your spouse, parents, emergency contact and beneficiaries. Once you are a member, we add your share capital, savings and loan records.

Your birth date, civil status, government ID numbers and financial records are sensitive personal information under the law.

Why we collect it

  • To evaluate your application and schedule your PMES
  • To keep your membership, share capital, savings, loans, interest on capital and patronage refund
  • To pay benefits to your named beneficiaries
  • To file reports required by the CDA, BIR and other agencies
  • To prevent fraud and protect your accounts

Who we share it with

Inside the cooperative, only staff and officers whose duties need it. Outside: regulators and authorities when the law requires (CDA, BIR, DOLE, courts, law enforcement on lawful order); NATCCO and insurers when your membership or a program you joined requires; and service providers that run our systems under contract and only on our instructions. We never sell your information or use it for third-party marketing.

How long we keep it

Only as long as the purpose and the law require: membership and loan records for the periods set by cooperative and tax regulations after your membership or loan ends. After that, records are securely shredded or deleted.

Your rights

You may ask to be informed, see, correct, object, or have your information blocked or erased when the law allows. You also have rights to data portability and to damages, and you can file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). We act on verified requests within 15 working days.
